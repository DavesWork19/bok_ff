### 1. Smack ’em and Sack ’em – Lance Taylor

Record: 6-1 / W8 Matchup: Favored by 33 over Preston Karr

**_Mr. Robot Machine, what do you think the record of this team is through 7 weeks?_**

Just looking at that roster, my gut says **4-3 through seven weeks**.

It feels like a team that would be **good, but annoyingly inconsistent**.

### Why I’d guess 4-3

-   **QB:** Drake Maye gives you a legit weekly ceiling.
-   **RB:** Ashton Jeanty + Breece Hall is a strong starting duo, with Alvin Kamara/Rachaad White as depth.
-   **WR/FLEX:** Zay Flowers + Christian Watson + Jaxon Smith-Njigba has upside, but I could see some volatile weeks here.
-   **TE:** T.J. Hockenson is solid, plus Tyler Warren gives you good depth.
-   **Bench:** Deebo Samuel is a pretty ridiculous bench option when available.
-   **Biggest issue:** This roster looks like the type that could score **135 one week and 92 the next** depending on touchdowns and WR production.

### My guesses

**Most likely:** **4-3**  
Second guess: **5-2**  
Third guess: **3-4**

If you told me this team was **2-5**, I’d say it probably got absolutely screwed by points-against 😂. If you told me **6-1**, I’d think Maye/Jeanty/JSN had been carrying hard.

**Final answer: 4-3.**
