### 1. Dak Lives Matter – Giovanni Franzese

Record: 4-0 / PF: 547 / Week 5 Matchup: Reed Pike

Sneaky Star: Parker Washington

Surprising Bust: AJ Brown (injured – not really a bust)

Gio is off to a blazing start this season – it seems everything he touches turns to gold. Some great pickups, reinforcements coming back from injury, and a high upside trade for Saquon Barkley has this team absolutely humming. Every player on this roster can go off for 20+ (some of them 30+ or 40+) and it’s not going to be fun lining up against this team. The advanced metrics for this team suggest that Gio is well on his way to tasting the sweet dried cum of the BK Fantasy Trophy for the first time. It would be an absolute shame if multiple players on this team starting getting seriously injured. Well done so far brother!

Record: 3-1 / PF: 483 / Week 5 Matchup: Jared Reiss

Dave! Dave! Dave! Dave! Dave! Rising up the ranks, and deservedly so. After a squeaker of a win against Lance in week 4, Dave is looking to keep riding high against another tough opponent this week. ### 8. Hakuna Nacua – Dillon Reisinger

Record: 1-3 / PF: 424 / Week 5 Matchup: Jake Odenweller

Sneaky Star: Denzel Boston

Surprising Bust: Colston Loveland

**MATCHUP OF THE WEEK ALERT!**

I’M PUTTING THIS OUT THERE – The analysts are extremely bullish on Dillon’s team. Last week we got a peak of what this team can be when healthy, and my god it looked scary.
