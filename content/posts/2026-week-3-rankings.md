-   **Bengals D/ST** = defensive countermeasures.
-   **The entire roster** = built around speed, athleticism, and splash plays rather than brute force.

**Personality of the object:**

> Sleek. Fast. Dangerous. Occasionally frustrating because the technology is so advanced that it doesn’t always perform at maximum efficiency, but when everything clicks, it can dominate anyone in the league.

Honestly, if this roster were an object sitting in a garage, it’d be a **black Lamborghini with a hidden nitrous system**. Looks good already, but the scary part is that Odunze, Henderson, and Love haven’t completely hit their final form yet.
