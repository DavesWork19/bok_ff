Below you’ll find our league specific bylaws – all other bylaws are in line with standard ESPN Fantasy Football rules and regulations.

1.  **FAAB TRADING IS NOW ALLOWED!**
    -   If you’d like to include FAAB in a trade, text the commission before the deal is done and let him know what money is involved – FAAB will have to be manually moved.
2.  **ESPN Default Injury Replacement**
    -   ESPN has now added a feature where they will automatically sub out an injured player in a starting roster for a player on your bench. I have turned this ON, as I was just manually doing this myself in the past. In order to have an injured player replaced, you must have a player on your bench that can fill in that spot. If you don’t have a player on your bench that can sub in – tough shit, you get a 0.
3.  **Fair Play Rule (or, Sherrick Rule)**
    -   We can’t have a repeat of the Sherrick situation, so we are implementing the Sherrick Rule this year. This rule states that each team must field a full lineup, with the intention of winning that week’s matchup (i.e. no 3 point projected player over a 16 point projected player). I know there’s some gray area here and anyone can argue about starting a player, but just use common sense. This was never an issue prior to last year, so I don’t see it being an issue going forward.
4.  **Last. Place. Punishment.**
    -   We are finally implementing a last place punishment. We’ve never done it before, so even playing field for everyone. Last place will be determined by the worst regular season record. If there is a tie, PF will be the tiebreaker. Gio and Drake have officially been named as the Punishment Squad and will be tasked with presenting ideas for punishment that the league can decide on. _DENIAL OF LAST PLACE PUNISHMENT WILL LEAD TO THAT INDIVIDUAL PAYING 3x ENTRY FEE THE FOLLOWING YEAR ($300)._
